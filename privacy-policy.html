Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026
Important Notice: This application protects your privacy. Health calculation data is used only for app features. The free app may show ads via third-party advertising services (uni-ad / Google AdMob). We do not sell your personal health data.

1. Information Collection

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Username, email address (if provided at registration or via Google Sign-In), and authentication credentials needed for login
  • Google Sign-In: If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google may share your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information with us so we can create or sign in to your app account. We verify the Google ID token on our servers. We do not receive or store your Google password
  • Health Data: Age, gender, weight, serum creatinine values — used only for calculations and history in the app, not for advertising
  • Calculation Records: Historical calculation results (for your convenience to view and manage)
  • Advertising / Device Identifiers: Separately from health data, advertising SDKs may use Advertising ID (such as Android Advertising ID / AD_ID) and related technical data to show and measure ads

2. Information Usage

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide creatinine clearance calculation services
  • Save and manage your calculation history
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Provide technical support and customer service
  • Display in-app advertisements and measure ad performance (via uni-ad / Google AdMob), using advertising identifiers — not your health calculation inputs

Your age, gender, weight, serum creatinine values, and calculation results are not shared with advertisers or used for ad targeting.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

This app may display ads provided through DCloud uni-ad, which may include Google AdMob and other ad partners.

When you use Google Sign-In, Google also processes authentication data under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can manage or revoke Google access in your Google Account settings. In-app you may delete your app account (Account settings → Delete account), which removes your app account data from our servers.

  • Ad partners may collect device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app activity related to ads, and diagnostic data, according to their own policies
  • On Android, the app may use the AD_ID (Advertising ID) permission for advertising and analytics
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in system settings (for example: Google Settings → Ads)

For Google’s privacy practices related to ads, see Google’s policies for AdMob / advertising services.

4. Information Protection

We take the following measures to protect your information:

  • Use encryption technology to protect data transmission
  • Restrict employee access to personal information
  • Regularly update security measures
  • Do not sell or lease your health calculation data to third parties

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored as follows:

  • Calculation data is primarily stored locally on your device
  • Account information is stored on secure servers
  • We regularly back up important data to prevent loss

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • View, correct, or delete personal information
  • Stop using our services at any time
  • Request us to delete your account and related data

7. Protection of Minors

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a minor, please use this app under the guidance of a parent or guardian.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. For significant changes, we will notify you within the app. Continued use of the app indicates your agreement to the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through:

  • In-app feedback feature
